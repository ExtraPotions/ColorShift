# Privacy

ColorShift runs as a local userscript in your browser. It does not send page contents, browsing history, diagnostics, or settings to a remote service.

Settings are stored in the userscript manager's local storage. Exported diagnostics and settings remain local unless you choose to copy or share them.

Site adapters inspect the current page only to apply colors, accessibility improvements, and the options you enable. No account names, profile identities, or personal content are collected by ColorShift.

## Reporting an issue

Please remove names, profile links, tokens, private URLs, and screenshots containing personal information before sharing a report. A minimal URL, selector, palette, and diagnostic finding is usually enough.
